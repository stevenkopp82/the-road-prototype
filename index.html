A Survival Card Game
The Road

It has been 10 years since The Collapse. We thought we were saving civilization, but we ended it instead. You survived, but supplies are running dangerously thin.

Somewhere beyond the wasteland lies The Sanctuary — a fortified refuge where survivors have rebuilt something worth protecting. You've heard the stories. You've chosen to believe them.

The road ahead is long. You'll scavenge what you can, fight what you must, and try to hold yourself together long enough to reach the gates. Food is scarce. Threats are everywhere. Every decision matters.

Your Objective Survive three legs of the journey — The Road, The Sprawl, and The Hive — and reach The Sanctuary before your health, sanity, or humanity runs out.

Overview

The Road is a solo survival card game. You play as a survivor navigating a post-apocalyptic wasteland, scavenging resources, fighting threats, and managing your health, sanity, and mutation across three dangerous locations before reaching The Sanctuary.

Each turn you reveal five cards on the Gather Track and choose one to take. Every choice costs food, draws threats, or both. The deck runs out — the journey ends. Make it count.

Components
Loot Deck — 74 Cards

The loot deck is split by location: 50 Road cards, 12 Sprawl cards, and 12 Hive cards. Cards contain four types: Gather (resources), Items (passive or active gear), Weapons (combat tools), and Vehicles (movement). Only Road cards are used in the first leg; Sprawl and Hive cards enter play through a draft at each location transition.

Threat Deck — 36 Cards

The threat deck contains 12 cards per location (The Road, The Sprawl, The Hive). When a threat cost is paid, the matching location's cards are drawn from a shuffled pile. The pile reshuffles automatically when exhausted. Types: Break (safe), Setback (stat loss), Monster (combat).

Mutant Deck — 9 Cards

When your Mutation marker crosses positions 4, 6, or 9 on the track, you draw a Mutant card. These grant permanent passive bonuses that stack across the run.

Setup & Difficulty

On the title screen, choose your difficulty. This determines how many Road cards are dealt into your starting draw pile:

ModeRoad CardsMeaning
Cautious30Shorter journey — fewer threats, fewer turns to find resources.
Resolute35Balanced. More road, more chances, more danger.
Desperate40The full wasteland. Maximum threats and turns.

Starting stats are always: Food 5 · Health 7 · Sanity 6 · Mutation 0. Five cards are automatically dealt to the Gather Track when the journey begins.

Cards beyond the deal count are permanently set aside — they never enter play. This creates variation between runs even at the same difficulty.

Threats & Combat
Threat Card Types
TypeEffect
☀ BreakSafe card — no effect.

Combat Resolution

When a monster is drawn, a battle screen appears. If you have a weapon with uses remaining, you may use it (adds its STR bonus) or fight bare-handed. Mutant bonuses (Forehead Tusk, Extra Arm, Hive Mind) apply automatically.

OutcomeResult
Your STR > Monster STRVictory — loot secured, no damage.
Your STR = Monster STRTie — loot secured, monster damage applied.
Your STR < Monster STRDefeat — loot lost, damage applied. The monster is injured (see below).
Injured Monsters

When you lose a battle, the monster is not fully defeated — it lingers at that slot with its remaining strength (monster STR − your STR). A 💀 badge shows on the slot cost. The next time you select that slot, you pay 1 extra food and must fight the injured monster first. Win or tie to clear it and proceed; lose again and it remains, weakened further.

STR Bonuses

Camouflage +1 STR in all battles. Holster +2 STR when using a ranged or explosive weapon. Forehead Tusk mutant +2 STR (stacks). Extra Arm mutant +3 STR with melee weapons only. Hive Mind mutant +3 STR in The Hive.

Card Types
Gather Resource Cards

Apply effects immediately and discard. Gather cards cycle back into the deck at each location transition.

Item Inventory Cards

Placed into your item slots (1 slot by default; 3 with a Backpack). If all slots are full, you must discard an existing item to make room. Items are passive or have limited active uses — see Item Reference below.

Weapon Combat Cards

Occupy a single weapon slot. Replacing a weapon permanently discards the old one. Weapons have limited uses; once depleted they are removed automatically. Three classes: Melee (high uses, moderate STR), Ranged (lower uses, high STR — reload with Ammo), Explosive (single use, very high STR).

Vehicle Movement Cards

Occupy a single vehicle slot and add their Move value to your SPD each turn. One use is consumed per turn the vehicle contributes to movement. Fuel Cans restore all uses. When uses run out, the vehicle is removed.

Mutations

Mutation increases from certain gather cards, monster defeats (ties and losses), threat setbacks, and Hive slot costs. All mutation is bad — the track moves in one direction only, toward game over at 10.

When your mutation marker crosses positions 4, 6, or 9 on the track, a Mutant card is drawn from the shuffled mutant deck. Each Mutant card grants a permanent passive bonus for the rest of the run.

Movement

After resolving a card and discarding the board, you move. Movement discards cards from the top of the draw pile — one per point of SPD. Those cards go to the discard pile and return in a future location's deck.

Base SPD is derived from Sanity. A Compass adds +1 permanently. A vehicle adds its Move value per turn (consuming one use each turn). Elastic Tendons mutant adds +2 SPD per copy.

If movement empties the draw pile mid-turn, the location transition triggers immediately.
Locations & The Journey

The journey has three legs. The gather board's border and background shift with each location.

LocationCardsFlavour
🛣️ The RoadRoad deck (30–40 based on difficulty)Open wasteland. Manageable threats, scarce resources.
🏘️ The SprawlDraft + discard pileSilent suburbs. More gear, more danger.
🏙️ The HiveDraft + discard pileDemolished city. High reward, high threat. Mutation costs on some slots.
🏛️ The SanctuaryWin conditionSafety — if you can reach it.
Location Transitions & The Draft

When the draw pile empties, a transition screen appears. For The Sprawl and The Hive, a draft follows: 7 location-specific cards are drawn from the pool and you choose 5 to keep. The 2 you don't pick return to the pool. Your new deck is those 5 cards plus your full discard pile, shuffled together. This is how Sprawl and Hive cards enter play.

Injured monsters are cleared on each location transition.

Winning & Losing
Winning

Exhaust the draw pile for the third time (completing The Hive) to reach The Sanctuary.

Losing

The game ends immediately if any of the following occur:

ConditionScreen
Health reaches 0You Have Fallen 🩸
Sanity reaches 0Lost to Madness 🌑
Mutation reaches 10The Change is Complete 🧬

Starvation (food at 0 when a food cost is owed) applies −1 Health and −1 Sanity simultaneously. Either can trigger a game-over condition. A Stash of Ration Packs will absorb starvation if available.

Use the Restart button (top-left of the game screen) at any time to return to the title screen and start a new run.
