The Road

It has been 10 years since The Collapse. We thought we were saving civilization, but we ended it instead. You survived, but supplies are running dangerously thin.

Somewhere beyond the wasteland lies The Sanctuary — a fortified refuge where survivors have rebuilt something worth protecting. You've heard the stories. You've chosen to believe them.

The road ahead is long. You'll scavenge what you can, fight what you must, and try to hold yourself together long enough to reach the gates. Food is scarce. Threats are everywhere. Every decision matters.

Your Objective Survive three legs of the journey — The Road, The Sprawl, and The Hive — and reach The Sanctuary before your health, sanity, or humanity runs out.
