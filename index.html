The Road

It has been 10 years since The Collapse. We thought we were saving civilization, but we ended it instead. You survived, but supplies are running dangerously thin.

Somewhere beyond the wasteland lies The Sanctuary, the last bastion of civilization left standing, and your last hope of lasting survival.

The road ahead is long, with danger on every side? Can you reach The Sanctuary before sucummbing to starvation, madness, injury, or the mysterious infection that turns men into beasts?

Your Objective Survive three legs of the journey — The Road, The Sprawl, and The Hive — and reach The Sanctuary before your health, sanity, or humanity runs out.
