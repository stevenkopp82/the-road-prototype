Official Rules
The Road
Goal Setup Turn Order Player Board Effects New Location Winning
Goal

You are a lonely traveler in a ruined world seeking out the shelter of the Sanctuary. Reach the Sanctuary alive, sane, and human to win.

Setup

Set out the player board and the gather board and separate out the following decks:

  • Loot cards — split into three piles: The Road, The Sprawl, The Hive
  • Threat cards — split into three piles: The Road, The Sprawl, The Hive
  • Mutation cards

Select a character. If the character starts with a card, find it in The Road loot cards and place it next to your player board.

Shuffle and draw 30 cards from The Road loot cards to form your travel deck. Place it next to the gather board. Select a difficulty and set your starting markers on the four tracks (food, health, sanity, mutation). Food cannot start above 8.

Turn Order

Draw 5 cards from the travel deck and place them in ascending order of card value left to right on the gather board (weakest left, strongest right). Select 1 card to loot, then resolve in order:

1. Pay Costs

Pay the cost shown on the slot. Move the marker on the player board down by the indicated amount. If the marker reaches the bottom of any track, take the appropriate action immediately (see Player Board Effects).

2. Draw Threats

Draw the number of threat cards shown on the slot one at a time. There are three kinds:

TypeEffect
☀ BreakYou dodge danger — no effect.
⚡ SetbackMove health, sanity, or mutation markers down as indicated. Apply Player Board Effects if any track bottoms out.
💀 MonsterEnter a battle. See combat rules below.
Monsters & Battles

Calculate your Strength (STR):

  1. Find your marker on the health track — the number to the right is your base STR.
  2. Modify by sanity: look for the STR modifier on the sanity track.
  3. Optionally add your equipped weapon's STR. Using it removes one use token. Weapons with no tokens cannot be used.
OutcomeResult
Your STR > Monster STRWin — monster discarded, loot secured, no damage.
Your STR = Monster STRTie — monster killed, loot secured, but take damage as shown on the monster card.
Your STR < Monster STRLose — take damage immediately. You may attempt one desperate lunge.

Desperate lunge: Subtract your current STR from the monster's original STR to get the monster's remaining STR. Roll the die. Compare the die result alone (no player STR) against the monster's remaining STR using the same win/tie/lose rules. If you lose a second time, you cannot try again.

If you lose a battle, do not draw any more threat cards that turn.

All threat cards are discarded after resolution. If the threat deck for the current location is exhausted, reshuffle it.

3. Get the Loot

There are four types of loot card:

TypeEffect
GatherAdjust player tracks as shown, then place the card out of play.
ItemPlace in an inventory slot (max 3). If full, discard one to make room. Cards with uses get tokens.
WeaponPlace in the weapon slot (max 1). Add tokens for uses. Discards old weapon if replaced.
ArmorPlace in the matching slot (Head, Body, or Accessory). Add tokens per the absorb value. Armor absorbs incoming damage instead of your tracks. Destroyed when all tokens are spent.
4. Cleanup

Place all unselected cards into the discard pile. If the travel deck is now empty, move to the new location (see Entering a New Location). Otherwise, begin your next turn.

Player Board Effects

Perform the following actions immediately when they occur.

Starvation

If the food track is at 0 when a food cost must be paid (or would drop below 0), move health −1 and sanity −1. You can still collect the card — starvation is the penalty, not a block.

Death by Injury

If health reaches 0, you immediately lose.

Madness

If sanity reaches 0, you immediately lose.

Mutation

If the mutation track moves onto a slot marked with a mutation icon (positions 4, 6, or 9), draw two mutation cards and choose one to keep. Mutation cards grant permanent bonuses. You may hold a maximum of three mutation cards. If the mutation track reaches 10, you immediately lose.

⚠ Sanity 1 triggers frenzy (+2 STR) — powerful, but one more Sanity hit ends the run. Armor absorbing sanity damage is critical at this threshold.

Entering a New Location

When you reach the end of The Road or The Sprawl (travel deck empty), perform the following:

  1. Draw 9 cards from the next location's deck (The Sprawl, then The Hive).
  2. From those 9 cards, select 6 to keep. Place the remaining 3 out of play.
  3. Add your 6 picks to your full discard pile and shuffle together. This is your new 30-card travel deck.

The cost overlay also updates to the new location and difficulty.

Winning

You win when you are in The Hive and can no longer draw cards from the travel deck — you have reached the Sanctuary.
